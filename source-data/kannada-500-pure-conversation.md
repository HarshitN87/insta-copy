## VERB ROOTS + SUFFIX PATTERNS (1–65)

1. **⭐present -thini (I do / will do) | add -thini to any root: maadu→maadthini, hogu→hogthini, baru→barthini / he does: -thaane, they do: -thaare | naanu naale barthini / avanu kacherige hogthaane, ninna manege hogi ammanavara jothe maathaadi, avaru thumba chinte maaduthaare, avarannu samadhaana maadu | I will come tomorrow / he goes to office, go to your house and talk with your mother, she worries a lot, console her**
2. **⭐past -dhe / -dha (did) | I did: root+dhe (maadu→maadidhe, hogu→hodhe) / he did: root+dha (hodha, bandha) / they did: -dhru | naanu chalanachitra nodidhe / avanu ooru hodha, male baruvaga munna eechara vahisi, angadige hogi tarakaari thandu, bega manege thirigi baa | I watched cinema / he went home-town, be careful before the rain comes, go to the shop, bring vegetables and return home fast**
3. **⭐negative -alla (don't / won't) | root+alla negates present and future: maadu→maadalla, hogu→hogalla, baru→baralla | naanu idu maadalla / avanu baralla, magu malagidaga jaasti galaate maadbeda, avanu eddhare aluthaane, swalpa sumane koothko | I won't do this / he won't come, do not make noise while the baby sleeps, if he wakes he will cry, sit quietly a little**
4. **⭐negative past -lilla (didn't) | root+lilla = did not: maadu→maadlilla, hogu→hogalilla, baru→bandilla/bandiilla | naanu thinnlilla / avanu bandilla, ninna thamma jothe shaalege hogu, avanige daari gotthilla, avananna kai hididu kaluhu | I didn't eat / he didn't come, go to the school with your younger brother, he does not know the way, hold his hand and take him**
5. **⭐-beda (don't! stop) | root+beda = prohibition: hogu→hogbeda, maadu→maadbeda, helu→helbeda | alli hogbeda! / jaasti maathaadbeda, nanna hendathi manealli kaayuthaa iddaale, naanu bega manege hogabeku, naale sigona | Don't go there! / Don't talk too much, my wife is waiting at home, I must go home fast, we will meet tomorrow**
